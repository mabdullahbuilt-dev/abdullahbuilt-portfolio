## Anchor policy

Use descriptive anchors such as “API integration development,” “market intelligence workflow case study,” and “MVP product development.” Avoid repeated generic anchors such as “click here.”

## Orphan check

All public sitemap URLs are reachable from a hub. Every detail page links back through breadcrumbs and forward to a commercial next step.
