# SERP and competitor gap analysis

Date: 2026-09-27

## Live research observations

- Current service competitors commonly foreground a packaged outcome, a specific customer, proof, delivery scope, and a direct next step.
- Strong product-development pages connect the visible interface to authentication, data, payments, administration, deployment, and ownership.
- Independent-builder pages can compete through inspectable live products and repositories where large agencies rely on logos and claims.
- Current AI-agent results reward dedicated service pages that explain tool integration, production controls, workflow automation, deployment, and safety boundaries.
- Current SaaS MVP cost results are mostly broad numeric estimates; Abdullah Built differentiates with a scope-first framework and does not publish unsupported price ranges.
- Build-versus-buy research increasingly emphasizes lifetime ownership, maintenance, security, opportunity cost, and strategic differentiation rather than initial coding cost alone.

## Gaps closed

1. Service pages now define buyer fit, deliverables, process, handoff, international delivery, relevant proof, and FAQs.
2. Work pages now expose screenshots, problem, constraints, role, architecture, decisions, validation, live demos, and source.
3. Guides now answer the question immediately and include navigation, evaluation frameworks, checklists, mistakes, and contextual proof.
4. Hub pages use CollectionPage and ItemList schema and descriptive internal links.
5. Contact now supports form, direct email, and booking paths without overstating email delivery.
6. A dedicated AI workflow service closes a distinct commercial-intent gap without creating thin keyword variants.
7. Three new guides answer separate buyer decisions and link directly to relevant services and public proof.

## Deliberately not implemented

- No unverified “X projects,” revenue, conversion, time-to-deliver, or client outcome claims.
- No fake US/UK/Canada offices or doorway pages.
- No Xroga case study because sufficient public evidence was not verified in the available source.
- No mass-produced keyword pages.

## Research limitation

Connected paid SEO tooling did not provide keyword volume, difficulty, or backlink data. Terms are therefore organized by observable commercial intent, not fabricated metrics. Re-rank priorities after Search Console impressions accumulate.
