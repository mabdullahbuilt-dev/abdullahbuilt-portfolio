About

02 / 04

I'm Muhammad Abdullah, an independent product engineer in Faisalabad and a hackathon winner. I work with founders and teams who need someone to shape the product and build the system behind it.

A brief doesn't have to arrive fully specified. I map the user flow, decide what belongs in the first release, connect the interface to real data and services, then test and ship. The same approach has taken me through market strategy tools, GitHub automation, contribution funding and cross-chain USDC products.

I take on complete product builds and defined engineering work for clients worldwide. If you have an idea, a prototype that needs to become reliable, or a hard feature in an existing application, tell me where things stand.
